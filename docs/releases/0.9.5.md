# WhyNavo 0.9.5

## 中文

WhyNavo 0.9.5 修复主页网站管理、图标编辑和空间导航遮挡问题，并进一步收敛主页图标的视觉形态。现有网站、文件夹、主页位置、Dock、笔记、任务和账号数据均会保留。

- “添加到主页/从主页移除”改为独立的主页清单，不再错误修改 Dock 固定状态；旧用户首次操作前继续看到原来的前 12 个入口。
- 主页编辑模式支持单击网站或文件夹直接编辑图标，拖动超过安全阈值时才调整位置，换图标不会再重置自由位置。
- 主页新增网站会优先加入“我的网站”；主页最多保留 12 个入口，达到上限时网站仍会安全保存到空间。
- 采集图标只有加载成功后才能选择；失效或分辨率不足的候选会明确标记为不可用，避免保存后又回退到旧图标。
- 导航与页面管理入口移动到设置上方的 `+` 按钮；保存后显示即时完成状态。
- 修复只更换导航图标时默认中文名称被误存为英文的问题。
- 空间和自定义空间增加左右对称安全区，左侧或右侧固定导航都不会遮挡第一列图标，同时内容中心保持不变。
- 主页图标使用柔和圆角、少量形态变化和更克制的慢速浮动；关闭浮动或系统减少动态效果时完全静止。
- 新的主页清单字段属于可选同步字段。为防止旧版严格校验拒绝或覆盖新版状态，最低同步版本提升为 0.9.5。

## English

WhyNavo 0.9.5 fixes Home-site management, icon editing, and navigation overlap in Spaces while refining the visual treatment of floating Home icons. Existing sites, folders, Home positions, Dock entries, notes, tasks, and account data remain intact.

- Add to Home and Remove from Home now maintain a dedicated Home selection instead of incorrectly changing Dock pinning. Existing users keep their first 12 visible entries until they customize the selection.
- In Home layout editing, click a site or folder to edit its icon; movement begins only after a safe drag threshold. Changing an icon preserves its free-position coordinates.
- Sites created from My Sites are added to Home when space is available. Home is limited to 12 entries; additional sites are still saved safely to Spaces.
- Collected icons become selectable only after loading successfully. Broken or undersized candidates are visibly unavailable, preventing a saved choice from silently falling back to the previous icon.
- Navigation and page management now opens from the `+` control above Settings and shows immediate saved feedback.
- Changing only a navigation icon no longer stores the English default name over a Chinese label.
- Spaces and custom spaces use a symmetric navigation safe zone, preventing either fixed side rail from covering the first icon column without shifting the visual center.
- Home icons use softer corners, subtle shape variation, and quieter slow motion. Turning motion off or enabling the system reduced-motion preference stops it completely.
- The Home-selection fields are optional synchronized data. The minimum synchronization version is now 0.9.5 so older strict clients cannot reject or overwrite the newer state.
