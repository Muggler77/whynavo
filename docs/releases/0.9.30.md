# WhyNavo 0.9.30

WhyNavo 0.9.30 integrates site creation into the Spaces icon canvas and makes the web-search provider control icon-only, clearer, and stable at every supported width.

## 中文

- 移除“空间”页面右上角独立的“添加网站”按钮，改为图标网格末尾的统一加号入口；空页面也能直接添加网站。
- 新入口沿用网站图标的尺寸、圆角和文字节奏，不增加额外的大卡片或悬浮工具条。
- 搜索框中的 Google 与百度切换改为纯图标按钮，文字保留在下拉菜单、设置选项和无障碍标签中。
- Google 与百度均使用本地 SVG 资源，避免文字截断和远程品牌资源加载抖动。
- 本版本不改变数据 schema、同步协议、备份格式或用户数据迁移路径。

## English

- The separate Add site button is removed from the top-right of Spaces and replaced by a matching plus tile at the end of the icon grid; empty Spaces remain actionable.
- The new entry follows the shortcut icon size, radius, and label rhythm without adding another large card or floating toolbar.
- Google and Baidu are now icon-only in the search trigger; text remains available in the dropdown, Settings, and accessible labels.
- Both providers use local SVG assets, avoiding truncated labels and remote brand-asset loading shifts.
- This release does not change the data schema, synchronization protocol, backup format, or user-data migration path.
