# WhyNavo 0.9.20

## 中文

- 包含 0.9.18 和 0.9.19 的隐私、R2 生命周期、最小权限和增量发布安全修复。
- 灰度部署现在会等待指定版本首页及其脚本、样式在 Cloudflare 自定义域名上保持一致，避免部分节点短暂混用新旧资源。
- 加强匿名访问烟雾测试的回归保护，固定检查真实的账号绑定同步 RPC 路径。
- `0.9.17` 在正式激活前继续作为唯一公开前序版本。

## English

- Includes the privacy, R2 lifecycle, least-privilege, and incremental-release safety work from 0.9.18 and 0.9.19.
- Staged deployment now waits until the release-specific home page, scripts, and styles propagate consistently through the Cloudflare custom domain.
- Strengthens regression coverage so anonymous-access smoke tests stay pinned to the actual account-bound sync RPC path.
- Keeps `0.9.17` as the sole public predecessor until final activation.
